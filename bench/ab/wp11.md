## Dedupe fixtures from the real channel CSV

```
$ venv/bin/python -m pytest -q illustrated_engine/tests/test_wp11_topics.py::test_dedupe_against_real_channel_csv_fixtures -v
```

Seeds history from `research/phase2/channel_videos.csv` (the real file, not
a synthetic fixture) and checks two real cases against it: a candidate
titled "Why Blood Falls In Antarctica Stains The Ice Red" is correctly
flagged as a duplicate of the real uploaded "Blood Falls in Antarctica"
(entity Jaccard 0.50 ≥ the 0.5 gate); an unrelated candidate ("The
Mathematics of a Perfect Soap Bubble") is correctly left unique. A second
test (`test_dedupe_series_exception_bypasses_entity_overlap`) proves the
§9.2 same-series exception: identical entity overlap is waved through when
`series_id` matches, and flagged when it doesn't — proving the exception is
real, not a blanket skip of the entity check.

## Score reproducibility

`test_score_candidate_is_reproducible_and_matches_the_weighted_sum`: the
same candidate + the same rubric row scored twice produces an identical
`(score, breakdown)` pair both times (pure function, no hidden state/RNG),
and the breakdown matches the exact §9.3 weighted sum by hand
(cluster_prior 25 + counter_intuitive 20 + visualisability 15 +
hook_strength 15 + series_potential 10 + evidence_strength 10 + evergreen 5
= 100 at full marks). A second test confirms an excluded cluster
(`crypto`) always scores 0 regardless of its rubric row.

## 20 candidates → ranked queue, no duplicate of existing uploads

Two complementary proofs:

**1. Fixture-seeded integration test**
(`test_build_queue_20_candidates_no_duplicate_of_existing_uploads`): history
seeded from the real channel CSV + V15 stories (138 real rows), a fake
`ask` returns 20 candidates with one deliberately set to the real
"Blood Falls in Antarctica" title. `build_queue(n=20)` correctly routes
that one out as a duplicate, scores and ranks the rest, and the resulting
queue has zero title overlap with the real seeded history.

**2. Live proof, real Claude calls** (`~/phase4_out/wp11/live_build_queue.json`):

```
$ venv/bin/python illustrated_engine/engine/v16_topics.py     # seed data/topic_history.jsonl (110 rows)
seeded data/topic_history.jsonl with 110 rows
$ venv/bin/python -c "from illustrated_engine.engine import v16_topics as T; import json; print(json.dumps(T.build_queue(n=20, ask=None, persist=True), default=str)[:200])"
```

Real `topic_ideate` + `topic_score` calls (claude_cli/sonnet), 2 LLM calls
total, $0.149, 93.1s: **20 raw candidates → 0 duplicates of an existing
upload → 4 rejected below `MIN_SCORE` (65) → 16 ranked into the queue**,
scores correctly descending from 93 ("Humans Are Closer to T-Rex Than
T-Rex Was to Stegosaurus") down to 66 ("Rogue Planets Wander the Galaxy
With No Star at All"). `data/topic_history.jsonl` grew from 110 to 130 rows
(16 queued + 4 rejected persisted, 0 duplicates to persist). Matches the
DESIGN §15.2 WP11 acceptance line exactly.
